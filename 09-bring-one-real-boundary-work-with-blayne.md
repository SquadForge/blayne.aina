## How to Evaluate Me: The 5-Step Live Enterprise Problem & Fleet Orchestration Protocol

Give me a sanitized or real, messy enterprise problem from your actual backlog—with full AI fleet access. An inconsistent CRM/ERP migration, brittle multi-system API integration, unverified document pipeline, or agent workflow that keeps producing bad state transitions will reveal more than a no-AI whiteboard performance.

Writing code by hand is no longer the primary bottleneck. The bottleneck is knowing what to build, decomposing it across parallel agents without context collapse, catching plausible-but-wrong outputs, and proving the result works. You do not hire an AI-native systems architect to out-type nine models. You hire them to make nine—or ninety—models produce something coherent, dependable, and verifiably correct against messy business reality.

**Watch and grade me on five concrete engineering dimensions:**

1. **Problem comprehension & operational discovery (the FDE lens):** How quickly do I cut through ambiguous stakeholder requests, inspect the underlying schemas/APIs, and identify the true operational constraints and failure modes?
2. **Bounded slice decomposition (preventing context collapse):** How do I break a system too large for any single LLM context window into independent, strictly typed work slices that parallel agents can execute without stepping on each other?
3. **Dependency, state & lease coordination:** How do I sequence dependencies across concurrent lanes, manage shared schemas/files, and prevent one agent's fix from breaking another agent's contract?
4. **Catching plausible-but-wrong agent output:** Clean-looking code and JSON can compile and pass superficial checks while hiding race conditions, silent data drops, or security bypasses. Watch how I interrogate outputs and force them through adversarial edge cases.
5. **Deterministic verification & refusal proof:** How do I establish—with reproducible receipts, before/after state hashes, and automated conformance gates rather than "it worked on my prompt"—that the system accepts valid inputs and deterministically refuses invalid ones?

**Plus a 15-minute live AINA runtime walkthrough:** Alongside your problem, I will share my screen and run `squad.exe` live—demonstrating a permitted transition, an approval-mismatch refusal (`requested_act_mismatch`), and a stale-worker lease refusal (`stale_lease_generation`) while walking openly through the architecture, tradeoffs, and what remains unbuilt.

The eight architecture papers ([§01](/01-an-ai-output-is-not-an-act/) through [§08](/08-sovereign-kill-switch-break-glass-epochs-and-conformance-gate/)) are the proof surfaces for that evaluation: per-transition standing, sealed evidence, protected computation and deterministic EISA adjudication, Room coordination, fenced ownership, forensic recovery, act pinning, crash continuity, emergency containment, and candidate-binary qualification.

### Track 3 — Protected Computation & Entity Resolution

**Who it fits:** enterprise data, RevOps, CRM, product-data, and integration leaders with duplicate contacts, companies, products, confidential identifiers, fragmented hierarchies, or unsupported canonical values.

**The asset:** one bounded contact, company, product, or other duplicate-record problem, or access to an existing replayable run. Initial scoping can use synthetic, redacted, or already authorized data; confidential production data is not required to start the conversation.

**The demonstration:** the privacy-first pipeline and its local return path:

- **Semantic Switch** transforms native values into structure-preserving fictional records before any provider-exposed computation. Native rows, keys, and the private crosswalk remain under client control.
- **qBlock / Qdrant Edge** consumes the protected records and nominates candidate blocks without declaring identity.
- **EISA Fracture** adjudicates those candidate partitions with explicit support, counterevidence, and preserved dissent.
- **EISA Forge** compiles canonical fields with source provenance rather than treating identity equivalence as permission to merge.
- **Local exact restoration** maps authorized field-source references back to native values through the private crosswalk; no model reproduces the originals.
- **Shield** handles later records through forward-only `DEFLECT`, `GROWTH`, or `ESCALATE` dispositions without rewriting the sealed baseline.

Semantic Switch is an experimental alpha relational-confinement module. The retained synthetic executions account for 2,750 inputs, 50 complete blocks, 2,704 fictionalized records, and 27,040 exact original-field restorations. Relation-isomorphism verification adds 20/20 relabeling-invariance blocks, 23/23 exact native edit relations, 53/53 records and 529/529 field values verified per run, 14/14 batch cases, and 10/10 transformation cases. These measurements establish **transport and relation fidelity on declared synthetic fixtures**—not population matching accuracy, anonymity, successful production merges, universal tokenizer preservation, or independent validation.

When evaluating entity resolution on a prospective customer's admitted dataset during a scoped pilot, transport fidelity is paired with explicit **matching-quality acceptance criteria**:

| Evaluation dimension | What a pilot measures |
|---|---|
| **Candidate recall** | Fraction of known duplicate pairs included in nominated candidate blocks |
| **Candidate precision** | Size and noise level of candidate blocks delivered to adjudication |
| **False merges** | Distinct entities incorrectly combined into a single partition |
| **False splits** | Records representing the same entity left separated without review |
| **Adjudication outcomes** | Proportion of records merged, kept separate, or held explicitly unresolved |
| **Field provenance** | Verification that every surviving canonical field cites its exact source record and pointer |
| **Privacy boundary** | Verification that raw identifiers remain local while external computation receives only protected fictional records |

**The boundary:** an existing implementation profile and acceptance run. The initial challenge excludes custom schema engineering, production CRM ingestion or write-back, lead-list generation, enterprise graph construction, and universal privacy or matching-accuracy claims.

Physical actuation, defense systems, and other high-consequence control surfaces do not enter a general-purpose focused challenge. They require separately scoped diligence and an environment appropriate to the consequence class.

## 2. Acceptance Is a Contract, Not a Demo Video

Before an accepted run, both sides write down what would count as success, refusal, and uncertainty. This prevents a positive anecdote from becoming proof of a different claim.

### Exhibit 9.2 — Demonstration and acceptance-contract template

```text
1. ENGAGEMENT AND DECISION OWNER
   Track, participants, technical owner, business decision owner, and authority to proceed.

2. ADMITTED INPUTS, PROVENANCE, AND HANDLING
   Synthetic/redacted/customer-supplied asset; who created the fixture and established expected
   positive, refusal, and unresolved outcomes before execution; source classification;
   permitted location; retention, network, credential, redaction, and return constraints.

3. EXACT TRANSITION
   Starting state → proposed act → governed execution or adjudication → terminal result.
   Name the implementation profile, destination, and evidence boundary.

4. PREDECLARED POSITIVE PREDICATES
   Observable conditions that must hold, such as exact readback, complete input accounting,
   expected citation resolution, or a forward-only baseline disposition.

5. PREDECLARED REFUSAL PREDICATES
   Hostile or stale cases that must fail, the expected typed refusal where applicable,
   and the destination state that must remain unchanged.

6. UNRESOLVED STATES TO PRESERVE
   Conditions that must remain in-doubt, carried forward, quarantined, or non-operative
   rather than being forced into success or failure.

7. ARTIFACTS RETURNED
   Agreed manifests, replay identifiers, receipts, view/case digests, refusal records,
   and any permitted recovery or conformance evidence, with redaction rules.

8. OPERATING BURDEN
   Latency, storage, availability, refusal, recovery, operator-load, and requalification
   observations relevant to this profile.

9. ACCEPTANCE RESULT AND CLAIM CEILING
   Satisfied / Failed / Unsupported / Not exercised / Inconclusive for each predicate;
   author-controlled vs. customer-supplied fixture provenance; explicit exclusions and owner duties.

10. NEXT DECISION
    Stop, narrow, repeat under the same scope, commission a paid pilot, or seek
    independent evaluation. No automatic production expansion.
```

This is an engineering acceptance template, not a claim that a demonstration is a legal service-level agreement or independent audit. Recording **fixture and ground-truth provenance** before execution ensures a reviewer can distinguish an author-constructed benchmark from a customer-blind test set and verify that expected results were fixed prior to the run. The returned artifacts are digest-linked and inspectable; a SHA-256 digest is not represented as a digital signature.

A failed predicate remains failed. An unresolved effect remains unresolved. A system that refuses every legitimate call has not passed a useful security test, so the contract includes both the permitted positive path and the hostile negative path.

### Minimum acceptance evidence by track

The generic contract becomes concrete before execution. The exact predicates are tailored to the admitted fixture, but a non-vacuous starting matrix is:

| Track | Positive predicate | Required refusal or rejection | Uncertainty that must survive | Minimum agreed evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Workflow standing** | The approved row reaches exactly the approved values and version +1, or the guarded file result matches the fixed proposal. | A changed act, stale tenancy, or superseded generation is refused without overwriting current state. | A crash between destination commit and receipt remains in doubt; a successor does not fabricate the original return. | `RequestedAct` digest, tenancy/generation evidence, recovery capture, exact readback, operation and reconciliation receipts. |
| **2 — Evidence and EISA** | Each citation resolves to its intended committed node and repeated evaluation preserves the expected verdict identity. | Drift, view tamper, invalid selectors, and false typed predicates receive their declared refusals. | Omissions and unknown coverage block unsupported absence; dissent and non-operative acts remain visible. | Source/view/case digests, omission inventory, served-content receipts, `ev_` handles, EISA submission and verdict records. |
| **3 — Entity resolution** | Every input is accounted for; an accepted replay restores the selected original fields exactly; later dispositions remain forward-only. | Altered release inventory, incomplete partitions, and unsupported field selections are rejected. | Unresolved records remain unresolved; candidate nomination is not identity proof and a graph path is not authorization. | Block manifests, release inventory, input/restoration counts, EISA lineage, canonical-field provenance, and Shield dispositions. |

These are **candidate predicates for scoping**, not a promise that every artifact applies to every run. The accepted contract names the actual subset and adds any profile-specific condition before the fixture executes.

**Technical acceptance is not a business-value measurement.** A fenced write does not prove revenue impact, and exact fictionalized transport does not prove matching accuracy. If a pilot has a commercial objective, write a separate value contract: baseline, metric and formula, population and observation window, data owner, decision threshold, and measurement owner. Without a baseline, the result is **Not measured**, not an inferred return on investment.

## 3. What the First Challenge Does Not Buy

Written qualification precedes scheduling. The initial discussion requires no confidential data and no platform commitment, but it must identify the question, handling constraints, participants, and decision owner.

A focused commercial challenge covers only the accepted existing path, preparation, operation, and explanation named in the written scope. It does not silently include:

- custom input conversion, schemas, connectors, adapters, or action profiles;
- production ingestion, integration, or write-back;
- enterprise-wide rollout or a new vertical deployment;
- relationship-graph construction, enrichment, or lead generation;
- unrestricted source access, repository transfer, or protected-material disclosure;
- independent validation, endorsement, praise, or a guaranteed conclusion.

Those are separate scopes. Customization and deployment belong to a paid pilot or implementation mandate. Independent evaluation follows the separate research route described below.

The article and ebook remain free. Commercial sessions, implementation, customization, deployment, and support are paid and privately scoped after qualification. There is no public fixed-price or fixed-duration promise because the admitted surfaces and acceptance obligations determine the work. Payment never purchases proof, an evidence-status upgrade, a favorable finding, or outcome control.

A commercial case record may remain confidential. If both sides agree in advance to publish a redacted, method-scoped record, it can report a positive, negative, refused, or unresolved result. Permission to publish is not a testimonial or endorsement.

**Evidence availability and measurement boundary:** implementation artifacts, accepted challenge packets, and supporting delivery records are `CONTROLLED` for qualified, method-scoped review, subject to source, customer, credential, and confidentiality limits. They are not unrestricted public downloads or independent validation. Latency, throughput, storage, availability, operator load, matching quality, and business value must be measured on the admitted profile; this article promises no universal performance envelope or return on investment.

## 4. Deployment: Small-Sufficient and Client-Controlled

A qualified deployment does not need to clone the author's full fleet or depend on a mandatory vendor-hosted AINA cloud. The reference deployment shape is an **AINA Portable Deployment Node**: a dedicated client-controlled small-form-factor computer or an on-site Windows reference environment, provisioned for the admitted transition.

Deployment is organized as four capability layers:

| Deployment layer | Delivered capability | Engagement role |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Existing Windows reference implementation** | **Reference runtime** | Runs the bounded Track 1, Track 2, or Track 3 challenge on controlled fixtures and produces inspectable receipts and refusal records. |
| **2. Repeatable deployment & qualification procedure** | **Release qualification** | Defines how a candidate binary, local ledger, and destination plan are provisioned, rebound, and checked against the 45-case conformance gate. |
| **3. Customer-provisioned Portable Deployment Node** | **Customer-controlled profile** | Adapts the smallest-sufficient reference profile to a specific customer's admitted hardware or VM, credentials, schemas, and target workflows. |
| **4. Supported customer-operated lifecycle** | **Operating lifecycle** | Establishes ongoing monitoring, connector-drift handling, `rsa_` reassessment workflows, and client-operated, founder-supported, or joint operations. |

There is no universal hardware specification. The smallest-sufficient profile follows the workload, evidence retention, provider dependencies, and consequence surface.

Controller-brokered model access keeps provider credentials outside the worker, while local state and artifacts remain under the client's custody.

Portability is deliberately bounded:

- **Software portability** means the qualified package can be provisioned in the declared environment.
- **Authority is not portable.** A copied lease, grant, approval, or Room identity does not automatically become valid at the destination.
- **State currency is not portable.** Local ledgers, generations, source views, and unresolved operations must be rebound and verified.
- **Network independence is not automatic.** Local execution can avoid a vendor-hosted AINA cloud, but an external model or destination still requires its declared provider route and terms.
- **Effect coverage is not portable.** Every new destination and alternate mutation path requires its own admission and evidence.

Deployment completion is not the arrival of files or the passage of a day. It means launch, local dependency binding, satisfaction of the written acceptance predicates, retained receipts, declared exclusions, and an operating owner. A narrow node may be brought up during a concentrated on-site engagement; there is no universal one-day deployment claim.

## 5. Operating Reality: Maintenance Is Part of the System

A governed deployment changes with its environment. Connectors and IDE adapters evolve. Provider APIs deprecate fields. Parser updates create new extraction identities. Schemas and destination policies change. New source views can require reassessment of earlier findings.

The operating model therefore includes monitoring, staging, affected-path replay, and requalification—not silent auto-upgrade.

### Exhibit 9.5 — Deployment and requalification checklist

```text
[ ] Destination authority, credentials, targets, and project roots rebound
[ ] Admitted model/provider routes and external dependencies declared
[ ] Source revisions, parser profiles, and view supersession rules recorded
[ ] Reassessment flags (`rsa_`) assigned to affected dependent findings/tasks
[ ] Connector/schema changes reflected in versioned destination plans
[ ] Alternate effect paths and new consequence surfaces admitted or excluded
[ ] Positive, refusal, unresolved, and recovery predicates replayed
[ ] Candidate source/image identity verified and 45-case gate passed before promotion
[ ] Latency, storage, availability, refusal, recovery, and operator load observed
[ ] Receipts, unresolved operations, exclusions, and recovery duties handed to an owner
[ ] Support model selected: client-operated, founder-supported, or joint
```

A source or requirement revision does not launder an old conclusion into a new context. Evidence-linked `rsa_` flags keep dependent findings and tasks marked for reassessment. A new parser identity creates a new view; historical handles remain tied to their historical view. Runtime changes require the candidate-binary publication gate again.

The client's responsibilities remain explicit: credential and key custody, protected local storage, provider entitlements, backup and retention policy, destination authorization, operating escalation, and any organizational control outside the software. Support can be client-operated, founder-supported, or joint, but it is not maintenance-free.

## 6. The Commercial Wedge: Duplicates to Defensible Relationships

Entity resolution is a strong first wedge because duplicate records are common, consequential, and evidence-rich. The problem is easy to describe and difficult to fake: which records represent the same entity, which values should survive, and what evidence justifies each decision?

The commercial expansion follows a disciplined chain.

### Exhibit 9.6 — From duplicates to distribution

```text
duplicate records
  → defensible identity substrate
  → canonical values with field-level provenance
  → evidence-linked relationships
  → inspectable graph paths
  → authorized read-only vertical pilot
```

The wedge is not generic “AI safety.” It is **entity resolution and evidence-linked state**. A resolved contact or company becomes more useful when deals, activities, tickets, addresses, parent organizations, and other associations can retain their provenance. HubSpot is one concrete CRM topology; Salesforce, Dynamics, NetSuite, and custom systems may present different authorized schemas and are not assumed to be identical.

An evidence-linked relationship graph retains source coordinates, relation type, scope, freshness, authority, and status for its nodes and edges. Unsupported paths remain candidate or unresolved. A configured search may include six-hop paths, but six hops are a **query horizon**, not proof of familiarity, influence, willingness to introduce, consent, or business value.

A graph path does not authorize outreach, disclosure, enrichment, mutation, or a CRM write. Those require separate authority and, where applicable, a controller-fixed destination act. Production graph ingestion and lead generation are separately paid scopes, not gifts hidden inside the initial challenge.

This creates a credible distribution path. A founding deployment partner can fund vertical customization. A distribution partner can contribute domain knowledge, qualified client access, and a repeatable channel. Neither label means sponsor, equity cofounder, or independent validator. Licensing, exclusivity, revenue sharing, support, data responsibility, and runtime rights require separate agreements.

The architectural differentiator is the common transition grammar—the layer that compiles organizational authority into per-transition technical standing so that evidence, actor, act, target, execution, effect, and closure remain bound even as model providers and adapters rotate. Client-controlled Windows execution is a differentiated deployment niche, not a claim that other platforms or a conventional composition cannot achieve equivalent properties. A mature stack that demonstrates the same bindings and failure behavior earns functional credit.

<h2 id="exhibit-9-6">Technical &amp; Security Due Diligence FAQ</h2>

These are the concrete mechanisms and assurance ceilings behind a technical walkthrough. Each answer links back to the relevant architectural proof surface.

<details class="faq-item" id="faq-q1">
<summary><strong>1. How can an already-authorized operation become a different operation between human approval and execution, and what precise mechanism prevents it?</strong></summary>

A high-level tool call can be approved at time <em>t1</em> and mutated before execution at <em>t2</em>: a path or image can be substituted, child arguments or environment state can change, or an inherited writable handle can address an unapproved target. AINA binds approval to a controller-fixed, RFC 8785 JCS-hashed <code>RequestedAct</code> before the worker environment exists. The worker is spawned suspended inside a restricted Job Object; the pre-resume audit observes its token, image and handle inventory before instruction 0. <code>requested_act::validate</code> re-resolves current task, source, tenancy, destination and act identity at execution. A mismatch such as <code>requested_act_mismatch</code> or an unclassified pre-resume capability fails closed; no resume or destination mutation occurs.

<p><strong>Proof:</strong> <a href="/07-defeating-approval-laundering-act-pinning-and-successor-grants/">§07 Act Pinning</a> · <a href="/proof/refusal/act-pinning.json">actual refused-substitution receipt and unchanged SQLite digest</a></p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item" id="faq-q2">
<summary><strong>2. What happens when an agent or local process already holds a writable handle to a file before the kernel lease is established?</strong></summary>

The lease cannot be granted. On Windows, the coordinator opens the target with <code>FILE_SHARE_READ</code> and without write/delete sharing. If another process already holds a compatible writable handle, the kernel rejects the coordinator's open with <code>ERROR_SHARING_VIOLATION</code> (os error 32). Acquisition fails closed, no generation token is issued, and the territory remains contested until the foreign handles are closed and the baseline is re-verified. The gate does not pretend that a clock expiry revoked a handle the OS still holds open.

<p><strong>Proof:</strong> <a href="/05-expiry-is-not-fencing-typed-leases-and-kernel-write-exclusion/">§05 Kernel Write Exclusion</a> · <code>os_lock_conflict</code> in <code>leases/oshandle.rs</code></p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item" id="faq-q3">
<summary><strong>3. What happens when the protected destination changes out-of-band after authorization but before final commit?</strong></summary>

Authorization binds a baseline file digest or SQLite row version; it does not authorize whatever bytes happen to exist later. At commit, file guards recheck the current preimage and tenancy while SQLite uses an immediate write reservation and a conditional <code>UPDATE ... WHERE version = expected</code>. A drift produces <code>preimage_hash_mismatch</code> or <code>record_version_changed</code> rather than an overwrite. Where candidate file bytes exist, divergence is preserved for forensic review rather than silently laundering it into a clean baseline. Quarantine is mechanism-specific, not a claim that every refused API call creates Amber bytes.

<p><strong>Proof:</strong> <a href="/03-eisa-four-gate-verdict-machine-and-entity-resolution/">§03 Deterministic judgment</a> · <a href="/05-expiry-is-not-fencing-typed-leases-and-kernel-write-exclusion/">§05 fencing</a> · <a href="/07-defeating-approval-laundering-act-pinning-and-successor-grants/">§07 fenced SQLite</a></p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item" id="faq-q4">
<summary><strong>4. What happens after a successful database commit or atomic rename followed by a crash before the receipt reaches the caller?</strong></summary>

The operation is treated as in-doubt, not blindly retried. Durable operation identities and pre/post execution records let recovery determine whether the effect committed. A fresh successor task can receive a narrowly scoped <code>result_read_only</code> grant to inspect the historical outcome after its tenancy, grant, source, delivery, and receipt bindings validate. The successor does not re-execute the mutation and does not fabricate the original worker's first receipt: <code>satisfies_original_return: false</code> and <code>resolves_original_obligation: false</code>.

<details class="faq-item" id="faq-q5">
<summary><strong>5. How does Semantic Switch preserve useful matching relationships without exposing native identifiers, and what are its actual limits?</strong></summary>

For structured CRM and logistics records, Semantic Switch replaces lexical identifiers with deterministic surrogates while preserving equality relations, edit-distance structure, blocking keys, and graph topology. A confined worker can perform blind entity-resolution tasks without receiving raw customer identifiers in its prompt. It is an experimental alpha relational-confinement layer, not encryption or universal anonymization. Tokenizer boundaries, unconstrained prose, frequency analysis, auxiliary-data correlation, and production matching accuracy remain outside the demonstrated guarantee.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I bring to an initial challenge?

Bring one bounded transition, its decision owner, an authorized synthetic/redacted/existing sample, handling constraints, and the positive and negative outcomes you need to evaluate. Do not send confidential production data or credentials with the first inquiry.

### Does a challenge include production integration or CRM write-back?

No. It covers the accepted existing implementation path and its evidence. Custom schemas, connectors, production ingestion, deployment, graph construction, and write-back require separately scoped paid work.

### Does a client-controlled node mean the whole system is offline?

Not automatically. There is no mandatory vendor-hosted AINA cloud, and local state can remain under client custody. External models, APIs, and destinations still require their declared network routes, credentials, terms, and evidence boundaries.

### Why is entity resolution the commercial wedge?

Duplicate contacts, companies, and products create a concrete, costly, evidence-rich problem. Semantic Switch lets external computation operate on structure-preserving fictional records while native values remain local; qBlock, Fracture, Forge, exact restoration, and Shield then create a defensible identity substrate. The pipeline does not guarantee matching accuracy or authorize graph-driven outreach and mutation.

### Does a paid pilot buy independent validation?

No. Commercial work is paid and privately scoped, but payment cannot buy praise, certification, an evidence-status upgrade, or outcome control. Independent evaluation uses a separate method-scoped research route and permits adverse findings.

### Do the regulatory mappings establish compliance?

No. They identify technical evidence that may support an organization's obligations. Use-case classification, jurisdiction, management systems, legal duties, DPIAs, SOC examinations, and conformity assessments remain separate organizational and professional work.
